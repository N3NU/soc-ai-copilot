TITLE: Privileged Access Policy

DOCUMENT_TYPE: policy

CATEGORY: privileged_access

DEPARTMENT: identity_access_management

SEVERITY: high

VERSION: 1.0

CLASSIFICATION: internal

LAST_UPDATED: 2026-05-24

PURPOSE:
Defines controls and requirements for privileged account usage.


INDICATORS:
- Shared administrator accounts
- Unauthorized privilege escalation
- Excessive permissions
- Inactive privileged accounts


INITIAL_ACTIONS:
1. Identify affected accounts
2. Review assigned permissions
3. Verify business justification


CONTAINMENT:
- Disable unauthorized accounts
- Remove excessive permissions


ERADICATION:
- Remove stale accounts
- Remove unnecessary privileges


RECOVERY:
- Restore approved access


COMMUNICATION:
- Notify IAM team
- Notify management


ESCALATION:
- Escalate unauthorized administrator activity


REFERENCES:
- IAM SOP v2.0
