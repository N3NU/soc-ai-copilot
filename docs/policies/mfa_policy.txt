PURPOSE:
Defines MFA requirements for enterprise systems.


INDICATORS:
- MFA disabled
- Repeated MFA failures
- MFA bypass attempts

1. Validate account activity
2. Identify affected users

- Enforce MFA enrollment
- Block unauthorized access

- Remove bypass mechanisms


RECOVERY:
- Restore account access


COMMUNICATION:
- Notify users


ESCALATION:
- Escalate privileged account violations
