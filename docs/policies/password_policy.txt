TITLE: Password Policy

DOCUMENT_TYPE: policy

CATEGORY: identity

DEPARTMENT: identity_access_management

SEVERITY: medium

VERSION: 3.1

CLASSIFICATION: internal

LAST_UPDATED: 2026-05-24

PURPOSE:
Defines password requirements and credential handling.


INDICATORS:
- Weak passwords
- Password reuse
- Shared credentials


INITIAL_ACTIONS:
1. Validate policy violations
2. Identify affected users


CONTAINMENT:
- Force password resets
- Disable compromised accounts


ERADICATION:
- Remove unauthorized accounts
- Revoke access tokens


RECOVERY:
- Restore legitimate access


COMMUNICATION:
- Notify users of reset requirements


ESCALATION:
- Escalate repeated violations


REFERENCES:
- IAM Standard v4.0
