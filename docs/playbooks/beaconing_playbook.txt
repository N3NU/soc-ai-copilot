TITLE: Beaconing Playbook

DOCUMENT_TYPE: playbook

CATEGORY: command_control

DEPARTMENT: security_operations

SEVERITY: high

VERSION: 1.0

CLASSIFICATION: confidential

LAST_UPDATED: 2026-05-24

PURPOSE:
Defines procedures for command-and-control beaconing activity.

INDICATORS:
- Periodic outbound traffic
- Repeated external connections
- Suspicious domains

INITIAL_ACTIONS:
1. Identify source host
2. Review network logs

CONTAINMENT:
- Block communication channels
- Isolate systems

ERADICATION:
- Remove malware

RECOVERY:
- Restore services

COMMUNICATION:
- Notify IR team

ESCALATION:
- Escalate active compromise

- C2 Detection Guide
