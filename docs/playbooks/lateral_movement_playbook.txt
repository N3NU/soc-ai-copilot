TITLE: Lateral Movement Playbook

DOCUMENT_TYPE: playbook

CATEGORY: lateral_movement

DEPARTMENT: incident_response

SEVERITY: critical

VERSION: 1.0

CLASSIFICATION: confidential

LAST_UPDATED: 2026-05-24

PURPOSE:
Defines procedures for suspected lateral movement activity.


INDICATORS:
- Remote service creation
- Unusual authentication events
- Pass-the-hash activity
- Administrative shares usage


INITIAL_ACTIONS:
1. Identify affected assets
2. Preserve evidence
3. Determine movement scope


CONTAINMENT:
- Disable compromised accounts
- Segment affected systems


ERADICATION:
- Remove attacker persistence


RECOVERY:
- Restore systems


COMMUNICATION:
- Notify leadership


ESCALATION:
- Immediate escalation required


REFERENCES:
- Incident Response SOP
