TITLE: Privilege Escalation Playbook

DOCUMENT_TYPE: playbook

CATEGORY: privilege_escalation

DEPARTMENT: incident_response

SEVERITY: critical

VERSION: 1.0

CLASSIFICATION: confidential

LAST_UPDATED: 2026-05-24

PURPOSE:
Defines procedures for privilege escalation activity.

INDICATORS:
- Unauthorized administrator access
- Unexpected role changes
- Privileged process execution

INITIAL_ACTIONS:
1. Identify affected systems
2. Determine exposure scope

CONTAINMENT:
- Disable accounts
- Restrict access

ERADICATION:
- Remove unauthorized permissions

RECOVERY:
- Restore approved privileges

COMMUNICATION:
- Notify leadership

ESCALATION:
- Immediate escalation required

REFERENCES:
- Privileged Access Policy
